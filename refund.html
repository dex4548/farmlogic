Refund Policy

Last updated: June 2025

✓ We offer a 14-day free trial — no payment required. Try before you buy.

1. Free Trial

All new FarmLogic users receive a 14-day free trial with full access to all features. No credit card is required during the trial. We encourage you to fully evaluate the Software during this period before subscribing.

2. Refund Eligibility

We offer refunds in the following circumstances:

  • Within 7 days of first payment — if you are not satisfied with FarmLogic after your trial ends and you are charged for the first time, contact us within 7 days for a full refund
  • Technical issues — if FarmLogic fails to function as described and we cannot resolve the issue within 14 days, you are entitled to a full refund of your most recent payment
  • Duplicate charges — any accidental duplicate payments will be refunded in full immediately

3. Non-Refundable Situations

Refunds will not be issued in the following situations:

  • After the 7-day window following your first payment
  • Renewal charges after you have been notified of the upcoming renewal
  • Partial months — subscriptions are billed monthly and we do not prorate partial months
  • Change of mind after the 7-day refund window
  • Inability to use the Software due to your own hardware or software limitations

⚠ To avoid unwanted renewals, cancel your subscription at least 24 hours before your renewal date.

4. How to Cancel

You can cancel your FarmLogic subscription at any time:

  • Log into your Paddle account at checkout.paddle.com
  • Navigate to your subscriptions
  • Click Cancel Subscription

Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period. You retain full access until then.

5. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund within the eligible period:

  • Contact us via GitHub: github.com/dex4548/farmlogic
  • Include your email address used for subscription and reason for the refund
  • We will process eligible refunds within 5-10 business days

Refunds are processed through Paddle back to your original payment method.

6. Subscription Changes

You may upgrade or downgrade your subscription plan at any time. Upgrades take effect immediately and you will be charged the prorated difference. Downgrades take effect at your next billing cycle.

7. Contact

For refund requests or questions contact us at: github.com/dex4548/farmlogic
