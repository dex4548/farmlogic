Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025

✓ Your farm data stays on your device. We do not collect or sell your personal farm information.

1. Who We Are

FarmLogic is developed and operated by Janwillem van Zyl, based in Namibia. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use FarmLogic software.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

FarmLogic stores all your farm data locally on your own device. We do not collect:

  • Your livestock records, animal data or herd information
  • Your worker details, salary information or HR records
  • Your financial data, transactions or bookkeeping records
  • Your farm locations or operational details

3. Data We Do Collect

We collect minimal data necessary to operate the service:

  • Account information — your name and email when you subscribe, processed securely through Paddle
  • Download statistics — anonymous download counts from GitHub (no personal data)
  • Payment information — handled entirely by Paddle, we never see your card details

4. Payment Processing

All payments are processed by Paddle (paddle.com). When you subscribe, Paddle collects your payment information directly. We receive only confirmation that payment was successful. Paddle's privacy policy governs how they handle your payment data.

5. AI Features

FarmLogic's AI Assistant sends your farm summary data (animal counts, financial totals, worker counts) to Anthropic's Claude API to generate responses. This data is not stored by Anthropic beyond processing your query. No personally identifiable information is included in these requests.

6. Automatic Updates

FarmLogic checks GitHub for software updates. This involves your device making a network request to GitHub's servers. GitHub may log this request as per their own privacy policy. No personal data is sent during update checks.

7. Data Security

Since your farm data is stored locally on your device, its security depends on your own device security. We recommend keeping your device password protected and making regular backups of your FarmLogic data.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your account information by contacting us
  • Delete your account and subscription at any time
  • Export your local farm data at any time from within the app
  • Opt out of the AI Assistant features at any time

9. Children's Privacy

FarmLogic is not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the application. Continued use of FarmLogic after changes constitutes acceptance.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests contact us at: github.com/dex4548/farmlogic
