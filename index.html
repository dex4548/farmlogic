Everything You Need

Built for Namibian
Farm Reality

No generic farm software from Europe or America. FarmLogic understands Namlits, Meatco, Agra, NamRA tax tables and Namibian farming conditions.

🐄
Livestock Management
Track cattle, sheep, goats, pigs and horses with full subcategories — Cows, Heifers, Tollies, Bulls, Oxen, Ewes, Rams, Lambs and more. Record births, deaths, sales and calf counts per cow.
📥
Namlits Import
Upload your Namlits herd statement CSV directly. FarmLogic reads every animal record and updates your herd automatically. No manual data entry needed.
🧾
NamRA-Compliant Payroll
Calculates PAYE using official NamRA 2024/25 tax brackets. SSC at 0.9% employee and 1.8% employer. Toggle PAYE and SSC per worker. Print payslips instantly.
📊
Bookkeeping & ROI
Track income and expenses per farm. Upload invoices and receipts — AI reads them and categorises automatically. See your ROI per animal and per farm.
🌾
Feed Calculator
Calculate exact feed quantities based on the correct 80/20 supplement to roughage ratio. Supports Enerfeed, Chop, Feedmaster, Molatek. Cost per animal per day.
🥩
Slaughter Records
Record Meatco slaughter grades A1, A2, A3, B, C. Auto-calculates dressing percentage and value. All Meatco abattoir locations supported.
💉
Vet & Health
Track vaccinations, treatments, deworming and check-ups per animal. Get alerts when animals are overdue. Never miss an FMD vaccination again.
🌿
Grazing Management
Track camps and paddocks with rotation status — In Use, Resting, Available. Monitor water points. Know exactly when each camp was last grazed.
🚜
Farm Implements & Job Cards
Track every tractor, bulldozer, padskraper and implement. Open job cards for repairs with auto-suggested part numbers for Ford, John Deere and Massey Ferguson.
🪪
Vehicle Licence Tracker
Track licence disc, roadworthy and insurance expiry dates for all vehicles. Get 60-day warnings per Namibian Road Traffic Act No. 22 of 1999.
📅
Farm Calendar
Schedule dipping, breeding, auctions, vet visits, maintenance and more. Set alerts 1 day to 2 months in advance. Vet and licence dates auto-appear from your records.
🤖
AI Assistant
Ask your farm anything in plain English. "What is my net profit?", "Which animals need vaccination?", "How is Farm 3 performing?" Get instant answers from your real data.
